QuietType 1.0 - private autocorrect for Windows 11

Run the installer. No administrator account, AI account, Python installation,
model download or internet connection is needed after downloading the installer.

QuietType opens Settings the first time. Click Close when you're ready.
The green Q stays in the Windows notification area (system tray). It may be
inside the hidden-icons popup opened by the ^ arrow beside the clock.
Right-click Q for Pause, Settings, Undo or Quit. Double-click Q for Settings.

Type an ordinary English sentence in a supported text box. After a space or
punctuation and a brief pause, clear typos are corrected. Uncertain words are
left alone. Closing Settings leaves QuietType running quietly.

Ctrl+Alt+P: pause or resume.
Ctrl+Alt+Z: undo the last correction, within 20 seconds and before changing
text or moving the cursor. That spelling is protected for this session.
If a shortcut is already used by another app, the tray menu still works.

Settings lets you change the delay, disable AI, exclude apps, protect words,
and disable automatic startup. Click Save settings to apply changes.
Real-word mix-ups are experimental and disabled by default.

No typing history is saved. No text is transmitted. No clipboard access.
Your explicit settings and personal words are stored in:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\QuietType
Uninstall from Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > QuietType.
Your settings are retained so reinstalling does not lose your preferences;
you can delete the folder above after uninstalling to remove them.

English only in 1.0. Some custom editors, games, elevated apps and text boxes
do not expose compatible accessibility information and are skipped.
Terminals, developer tools and common password managers are excluded by default.
Passwords, selected text, read-only text, addresses and code-like words are skipped.

QuietType is not a guarantee about Windows or other apps' own data handling.

This community build is unsigned. Windows may show an unknown-publisher or
SmartScreen notice because it has no paid signing certificate or reputation.
The release includes SHA256SUMS.txt; source and build steps are public.
